**Essential (must have):** full name, claim type, DOI, mechanism. **Important (ask if not mentioned):** DOB, contacts, employer (WC/TPD), work status, treating doctor. **Don't ask (fill later):** insurer claim number, specific diagnoses, WPI, CoC details, settlement value.
**Per claim type:** WC: claim lodged? accepted/rejected? weekly payments? CoC? employer? | TAC: accident type? at fault? no-fault benefits? | PL: where? occupier? witnesses? photos? | TPD: fund? off work since? occupation?
