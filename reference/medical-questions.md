# Medical Questions
## General (all reports)
1. Diagnosis(es) 2. Prognosis 3. MMI reached? When expected? 4. Current work capacity (full/partial/nil, hours, restrictions) 5. Current treatment 6. Recommended treatment 7. Is treatment reasonable and necessary?

## Causation
8. Causally related to injury? 9. If partial: percentage attribution (injury vs pre-existing vs other) 10. Significant contributing factor? (s40 WIRC Act threshold)

## Impairment (when relevant)
11. WPI using AMA 4th Edition 12. Separate physical/psychiatric WPI 13. Chapter/table references

## Psychological
14. DSM-5 diagnosis 15. GAF score 16. Direct result or secondary to physical? 17. Pre-existing psych conditions? 18. Treatment recommendation (type, frequency, duration)

## WorkCover-specific
19. Current work capacity (WIRC Act definition)? 20. Suitable employment capable? 21. Specific duties/hours for partial capacity? 22. RTW barriers?

## TAC-specific
23. Consistent with transport accident? 24. WPI exceeds threshold (30% P&S / 10% pecuniary)?

## Body-part additions: Spinal (ROM, neuro, imaging) | Upper limb (grip, ROM) | Lower limb (gait, weight-bearing) | Psychiatric (MSE, PHQ-9, GAD-7, PCL-5, functional impact)
