# Comcare — Safety, Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 1988 (Cth)

## Jurisdiction
Federal. Covers Commonwealth employees and employees of licensed self-insurers.
NOT governed by Victorian WorkCover legislation. WIC has NO jurisdiction.

## Key Sections
| Topic | Section | Use |
|-------|---------|-----|
| Injury definition | s4 | Must arise out of or in course of employment |
| Disease definition | s5B | Contributed to in material degree |
| Employee notice | s53 | Notice to employer of injury |
| Claim lodgement | s54 | Claim for compensation |
| Liability determination | s61 | Comcare/licensee determines — up to 6 months |
| Incapacity payments | s19 | Normal weekly earnings basis |
| Medical treatment | s16 | Reasonable and necessary medical treatment |
| Household services | s29 | Attendant care / domestic assistance |
| Permanent impairment | s24 | Lump sum — 10%+ WPI required |
| Common law election | s44 | Must elect — threshold 10% WPI |
| Reconsideration | s62 | Internal review of determination — 30 days |
| AAT review | s64 | Administrative Appeals Tribunal — 60 days from reconsideration |

## Claim Process
1. Employee notifies employer of injury (s53)
2. Claim lodged with Comcare or licensed self-insurer (s54)
3. Liability determined (s61) — up to 6 months (often much longer in practice)
4. Accept: incapacity payments + medical commence
5. Reject: internal reconsideration (s62, 30 days) → AAT review (s64, 60 days from reconsideration)

## Key Differences from Victorian WorkCover
- Federal jurisdiction — Victorian WIC/WorkSafe has NO jurisdiction
- No conciliation at WIC — review pathway is s62 reconsideration → AAT
- Different incapacity payment calculation (normal weekly earnings, not PIAWE)
- Comcare determines liability, not a WorkSafe agent
- Licensed self-insurers (e.g., Australia Post, Telstra, FedEx Express Australia) handle own claims
- Different WPI assessment methodology
- Common law election threshold: 10% WPI (s44)
- No serious injury narrative test — threshold is WPI-based only

## Licensed Self-Insurers (common ones)
Australia Post, Telstra, Australian Defence Force, FedEx Express Australia, Optus, Linfox

## Limitation
Common law: 3 years from date of injury or knowledge (Limitation of Actions Act 1958 if proceeding in Vic).
Check jurisdiction — may need to proceed in Federal Court or ACT Supreme Court.
