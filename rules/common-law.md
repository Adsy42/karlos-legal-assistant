# Common Law Pathway

## WorkCover (WIRC Act s134AB)
Prerequisites: accepted/disputed claim + serious injury certificate + election
Serious injury: narrative test, County Court application, s134AB(16)-(38)
Election: irrevocable (s134AB(5)), weekly payments continue 52 weeks post-election

## TAC (TAC Act s93)
Prerequisites: transport accident + impairment threshold (>30% WPI P&S / >10% pecuniary) OR serious injury certificate (s93(4))

## Public Liability
Prerequisites: duty, breach, causation, damage + significant injury threshold (s28LE Wrongs Act)

## Damages available (all): general damages, past/future economic loss, past/future medical, domestic assistance, Fox v Wood (tax), Griffiths v Kerkemeyer (gratuitous care)
