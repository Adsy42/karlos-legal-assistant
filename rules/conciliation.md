# Conciliation — Workplace Injury Commission

**Application:** s257 WIRC Act. 60 days from rejection/termination. No extension. No fee.
**Process:** Application → WIC allocates conciliation officer → conference (4-8 weeks) → agreement or no agreement
**Outcomes:** Agreement (binding) | No agreement → genuine dispute certificate → Medical Panel (s270) or court
**Preparation:** chronology, medical reports, insurer decision, CoC, wage records, pre-conciliation submission
