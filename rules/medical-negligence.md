# Medical Negligence — Wrongs Act 1958 (Vic) + Common Law

## Key Sections
| Topic | Section | Use |
|-------|---------|-----|
| Duty of care | s48 Wrongs Act | Standard of reasonable care |
| Professional standard | s59 Wrongs Act | Peer professional opinion (modified Bolam) |
| Causation | s51 Wrongs Act | Factual causation + scope of liability |
| Obvious risk | ss53-54 Wrongs Act | Defence — but limited in medical context |
| Significant injury | s28LE Wrongs Act | Non-economic loss threshold (narrative test) |
| Non-economic loss cap | s28LF Wrongs Act | Damages cap |
| Contributory negligence | ss62-63 Wrongs Act | Reduction (rarely applies in medical) |
| Limitation (3yr) | s5(1AA) Limitation Act | 3 years from discoverability |
| Discoverability | s27D Limitation Act | When patient knew/should have known |
| Long-stop (12yr) | s27K Limitation Act | Absolute bar |
| Pre-court notice | Part VBA Wrongs Act | Mandatory pre-litigation steps |

## Process
1. Obtain complete medical records from treating facility (FOI if public hospital)
2. Obtain expert liability report (independent specialist — same discipline as defendant)
3. Assess quantum — expert evidence on injuries, causation, prognosis
4. Serve pre-court notice under Part VBA Wrongs Act (MANDATORY before proceedings)
5. Defendant has 90 days to respond with position on liability
6. Attempt resolution — mediation or negotiation
7. If no resolution: issue proceedings (usually County Court or Supreme Court)
8. Expert evidence required at trial — joint expert conferences typical

## Key Differences from Other PI
- Expert liability report is essential BEFORE proceedings — cannot proceed without it
- Pre-court notice under Part VBA is MANDATORY — proceedings without it are premature
- Defendant is usually a hospital/health service, not an employer or insurer
- Public hospitals insured by VMIA (Victorian Managed Insurance Authority)
- Private hospitals/practitioners have private medical indemnity insurance
- Limitation is discoverability-based — when patient knew or should have known of injury
- No insurer/employer/WorkSafe paradigm
- Damages: general damages, past/future economic loss, medical, domestic assistance, Fox v Wood
- Same significant injury threshold as public liability (s28LE Wrongs Act)

## Defendant Insurers
- Public hospitals (e.g., Northern Health, Western Health): VMIA
- Private practitioners: Medical indemnity providers (MIGA, MDA National, Avant, MIPS)
- Private hospitals: Commercial insurers
