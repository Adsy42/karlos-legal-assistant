# Medical Panels Victoria

**Jurisdiction:** s270 WIRC Act / s46 TAC Act. Questions of medical fact. Referral from conciliation officer, court, or consent.
**Questions:** nature/extent of injury, capacity, causation, WPI, treatment reasonableness, MMI
**Process:** referral with questions → panel constituted (2-3 specialists) → worker examined → opinion issued
**Binding:** cannot be appealed on merits. Only challenged on jurisdictional grounds (judicial review).
