# TPD — Total and Permanent Disability

Fund-specific. Not statutory. Governed by fund deed + Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth).

**Definitions:** Own Occupation (can't do own job) | Any Occupation (can't do any job) | ADL (can't do daily activities)

**Process:** 1. Obtain fund deed/PDS 2. Confirm waiting period (3-6mo) 3. Medical evidence addressing specific definition 4. Lodge through trustee 5. Insurer recommends 6. Trustee decides

**Key refs:** s13 Insurance Contracts Act (utmost good faith) | s52 SIS Act (trustee obligations) | AFCA (complaints)

**Limitation:** 6 years from cause of action (contract). Check fund deed for internal deadlines.
