## Claim Process
1. Worker completes WorkSafe Claim Form
2. Employer forwards to insurer within 10 days (s107)
3. Insurer decides within 28 days (s103). Not decided = deemed accepted (s103(2))
4. Accept: weekly payments + medical commence. Reject: conciliation within 60 days (s257)

## Weekly Payment Rates
- First 13 weeks (s165): 95% PIAWE
- 13-52 weeks (s166): 80% PIAWE
- After 52 weeks (s167): 80% if current work capacity. Must demonstrate ongoing incapacity
- After 130 weeks (s168): cease unless no current work capacity AND likely indefinite

## Medical Expenses (s114)
Pre-approval required. Insurer: 10 business days to respond. No response = deemed approved.

## Impairment Benefits (ss98A-98D)
MMI required. AMA 4th Edition. Min 10% WPI. Psychiatric assessed separately.

## Common Law (s134AB)
Serious injury certificate required (narrative test). Election irrevocable (s134AB(5)). Weekly payments continue 52 weeks post-election.

## Conciliation (s257)
60 days from rejection/termination. No extension. Filed at WIC. Not binding unless agreement reached.
